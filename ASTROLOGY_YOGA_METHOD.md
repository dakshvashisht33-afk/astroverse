# AstroVerse yoga screening: method and scope

AstroVerse treats a yoga as a traditional chart rule to investigate, not as a guaranteed event or a ranking of a person's life. The current implementation screens selected combinations in the sidereal D1 (Rashi) chart. It reports the trigger, involved planets/houses, interpretive theme, and factors that need review. The AI receives that same screen and must not invent additional yoga matches.

## Currently screened

- Kendra–Trikona and Dharma–Karmadhipati Raja Yoga relationships.
- Pancha Mahapurusha combinations (Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Shasha).
- Common Viparita Raja Yoga screens (Harsha, Sarala, Vimala).
- Selected Dhana Yoga connections between wealth-house and trikona lords.
- Gaja Kesari, Budha–Aditya, Chandra–Mangala, Adhi, and Amala screens.
- Neecha-bhanga indicators, explicitly separated from a confirmed Neecha-bhanga Raja Yoga.
- Separate dosha/transit screens for Mangal Dosha, Kaal Sarpa, and Sade Sati.

## Interpretation safeguards

1. A rule match is not a strength score. Review dignity, functional lordship, exact degrees, combustion, retrogression, aspects, conjunctions, affliction, divisional support, and relevant dasha before drawing a conclusion.
2. Birth-time-dependent rules are withheld when birth time is unknown. Moon-based findings may still be available, with their method stated.
3. A missing match means only that the implemented screen did not trigger. It does not mean that no tradition recognizes a related combination.
4. Schools and editions differ in yoga definitions, exceptions, cancellations, and chapter numbering. References below point to the tradition or textual topic, not a claim that this short rule set exhausts a text.
5. Do not turn astrology into medical, legal, financial, or other consequential guarantees.

## Reference starting points

- [Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 39 (Raja Yoga)](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf) — a searchable translation; chapter numbering and translations vary by edition.
- [BPHS chapter 39, verse-oriented translation](https://vedicpupil.in/library/books/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/chapter-39) — useful for comparing specific rule formulations.
- [Comparative BPHS text and translations](https://saptamatrika.ru/en/bphs) — presents multiple editions/translations for comparison.

This document describes a deliberately bounded implementation. It is not an extraction or substitute for the complete books listed by the project owner, and the present screening should not be described as exhaustive.
